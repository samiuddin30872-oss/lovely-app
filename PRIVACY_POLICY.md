# Privacy Policy — Lovely Enterprises App

**Last updated: September 2026**

Lovely Enterprises ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect
This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers. The app simply displays our website (lovelyenterprises' service page) inside the app.

- We do not require login or account creation.
- We do not collect your name, location, or personal data through the app itself.
- If you choose to call us or message us on WhatsApp using the buttons in the app, that call or chat happens directly through your phone's own Call/WhatsApp app — subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
- If you choose to select a photo or video to send us (for a service request), that file stays on your device and is only shared if you personally choose to send it to us via WhatsApp — we do not automatically upload or access your photos/videos.

## Permissions Used
- **Internet** — to load our service information.
- **Phone (Call)** — only when you tap "Call Now"; opens your phone's own dialer.
- **Camera / Photos (optional)** — only if you choose to attach a photo/video to send us your service request.
